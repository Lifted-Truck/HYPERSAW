Task #23 — the integrated reverb. Built as a full signal chain rather than a bare FDN, because the "acoustic strings section" quality the human heard from an external reverb most likely lives in the early reflections and diffusion, not in the tail length. Also tests the roadmap's open question: does the reverb get its OWN SWARM (coherent modulation, in the instrument's own idiom) or stay a static space? Audition lab, spec-in-code — NOT a reference. Keys A..;, Z/X octave, space drone.

Space

Decay is set as a time and converted to per-line feedback, so changing size reshapes the room without changing how long it rings.

Early reflections

12 panned taps over the first ~80 ms. This is the hypothesis under test: that the "acoustic strings section" quality comes from the early pattern, not the tail. Pull ER send to 0 and the room should stop sounding like a place.

Modulation — the swarm question

Static FDN lines ring metallically; movement breaks it up. K = 0 is the conventional answer — eight independent drifts. As K → 1 they lock into ONE coherent breath: the instrument's own idiom applied to its room. The roadmap asks which is right; this is where you decide.
