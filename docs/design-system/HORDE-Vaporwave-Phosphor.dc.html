Trade-offs, honestly: contrast between cream chrome and dark tubes makes data pop hard and gives every well a bezel for free — but the page gains a second world (two grounds to balance), the sunset gradient breaks the flat-fills-only rule (one gradient, level-mapped, quarantined to spectrum bars), and glow costs an underlay stroke per trace in the native painter (cheap — still no blur). Interop: value/meter/physics/marker keep their MEANINGS, only their hexes re-tune for emission (#FF5ECF/#59F6E8/#9D6CFF/#FFB86B). If adopted, this ships as a WELL-ONLY token swap: 5 screen tokens + a trace-rendering mode, zero layout change — and it composes with the aesthetic-collapse idea as a corner skin.
